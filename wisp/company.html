Company

We build the way out.

The tools people carry for protection were designed for fighting. We think the best outcome of any confrontation is that everyone goes home, starting with you.

01 · Mission

Make leaving the easiest thing to do when someone raises a weapon. No injuries, no escalation, no split-second decisions about force.

01

Leaving is winning

We measure success by how quickly the wearer is safe, not by what happens to the attacker.

02

Hardware you forget

Protection only works if it's there. Wisp has to be light and comfortable enough to wear every day.

03

Trust is a feature

Privacy, logging and safety limits are product requirements with owners and tests, not policies.

04

Earn it in the field

We test with professionals before anyone else, and we publish what we learn about safety.

02 · Where we are

Flying prototypes.

W1 is our first complete system: drone, harness and flight software, flying in the lab.

  1. Now

    W1 prototype

    Flying prototypes, the third harness revision, and light and flight testing in the lab.

  2. Next

    Field trials

    Supervised trials with professional security teams, and the first independent safety tests.

  3. Then

    Certification

    Regulatory approvals and a production design, before Wisp is offered to anyone.
