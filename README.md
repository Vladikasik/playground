# playground

## Wisp drone standoff

`wisp-drone-scene/index.html` is a self-contained 3D scene (Three.js, loaded from a CDN) that imagines a backpack-launched deterrent drone:

1. Two people stand 3.6 m apart on a night street.
2. The attacker draws a pistol from the waistband and aims.
3. The defender stays calm and taps a strap button.
4. The backpack hatch pops and a palm-sized ducted drone lifts out.
5. It flies over the defender and locks onto the attacker's face.
6. It orbits the attacker's head about half a meter out, flooding the eyes with light.
7. The defender backs off and walks away while the drone keeps circling (3–5 min flight time).

Open the file in a browser (it needs network access for the Three.js CDN and fonts). Controls: play/pause, timeline scrubbing, half speed, four cameras (directed film, free orbit, the drone's camera, the attacker's view), and a spec sheet.

The light defaults to a slow soft pulse. The real ~11 Hz strobe is opt-in behind a photosensitivity warning.

All drone figures are illustrative for the scene, not a real product.
